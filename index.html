Open 24 hours daily · Unity Estate, Fagge LGA

Yes. Maria Ville Specialist Clinic & Maternity operates 24 hours daily from Monday through Sunday, with 10 inpatient beds for routine maternal and general primary care admissions. You may call +234 806 029 6224 ahead of your visit.

On-site, we provide Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Antenatal Care (ANC), Family Planning, Paediatrics & Immunisation, General Medical & minor Surgical care, and HIV/AIDS testing and counselling support.

Because we are a licensed Primary Health Care Centre without an on-site laboratory, pharmacy, imaging suite (ultrasound/X-ray), ambulance, dental, or mortuary unit, diagnostic tests and prescriptions are completed via external centres, and high-risk pregnancies or complex deliveries are referred promptly to a secondary or tertiary hospital.

Yes. We are listed as a provider on Hygeia HMO (Provider ID: 5095). For self-pay visits and fees, we accept debit cards, credit cards, and contactless NFC mobile payments. Please call reception to confirm current consultation or antenatal rates.

We are located at No. 7B Unity Estate, Jaba/Panisau Road, Sabon Gari East (Gwagwarwa), Fagge LGA, Kano 700211. Both the clinic entrance and parking area are wheelchair accessible.
